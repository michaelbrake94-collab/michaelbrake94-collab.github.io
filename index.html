Hobbs

Hobbs is a private personal assistant and coach that one person built for their own use. It runs on their home computer and is not available to anyone else. There is nothing to sign up for or download.

With its owner's permission, Hobbs connects to the owner's own Gmail account so it can summarise and search their email, save drafts for them to review, sort email with their labels, and move labelled email out of their inbox, and move email they choose to Trash. It does not send email or delete it permanently.

Hobbs can also connect to the owner's own Google Calendar, so it can show their schedule, book events and reminders, and colour-code events. It does not add guests or send invites.

Hobbs can also connect to the owner's own Google Drive to find and read their documents. It cannot create, change, share or delete anything there.
