Hobbs privacy policy

Hobbs is a private assistant with one user, its owner. It is not offered to anyone else, so the only Google account it handles is the owner's own.

What Hobbs accesses

When the owner asks, Hobbs reads and searches email in the owner's Gmail account, saves drafts and adds or removes the owner's labels. It does not send, archive, trash or delete email.

Where the data goes

The Google sign-in token and Hobbs's conversation log are stored on the owner's home computer. To answer a request, the email content involved is sent to Anthropic's Claude service under the owner's own Claude account. Email content is not sold, used for advertising or shared with anyone else.

Google user data

Hobbs's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can end Hobbs's access at any time from their Google account permissions, and can delete the stored token and conversation log from their computer.
