Hobbs privacy policy

Hobbs is a private assistant with one user, its owner. It is not offered to anyone else, so the only Google account it handles is the owner's own.

What Hobbs accesses

When the owner asks, Hobbs reads and searches email in the owner's Gmail account, saves drafts adds or removes the owner's labels, moves labelled email out of the inbox, and moves email the owner chooses to Trash. It does not send email or delete it permanently.

When the owner asks, Hobbs also reads events in the owner's main Google Calendar, including the names, email addresses and replies of the people on them, books events, changes or deletes events it made, and changes the colour of events. After the owner approves each one, it sends invites, changes and cancellations to the guests the owner chooses, and Google emails those guests the event's details.

When the owner asks, Hobbs also searches and reads documents in the owner's Google Drive, including files shared with them. It cannot change, share or delete what is already there. Once the owner approves each batch, Hobbs saves attachments from their email into a folder of its own in their Drive.

Where the data goes

The Google sign-in tokens, Hobbs's conversation log and its list of the owner's open decisions, which can include details from email, calendar or documents, are stored on the owner's home computer. To answer a request, the email, calendar or document content involved is sent to Anthropic's Claude service under the owner's own Claude account. This content is not sold, used for advertising or shared with anyone else.

Google user data

Hobbs's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can end Hobbs's access at any time from their Google account permissions, and can delete the stored tokens and conversation log from their computer.
